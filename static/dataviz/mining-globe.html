Cheat sheet: how to read a miner

Producers dig and sell metal, so profits swing with the commodity price. Royalty and streaming firms fund mines in return for a slice of output, with lower costs and steadier cash. Juniors explore and are far riskier. All-in sustaining cost (AISC) is what it costs to produce an ounce or pound; the wider the gap to the metal price, the fatter the margin. Miners tend to move with a bigger beat than the metal itself, up and down.
