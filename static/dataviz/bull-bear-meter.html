What’s driving the reading

How it works: each signal is scored 0 (fully bearish) to 100 (fully bullish), then averaged with equal weight. Signals are calculated from daily price and volume history (plus the VIX, the yield curve and credit spreads for US indices), with wider bands for choppier assets like crypto. A 20% fall from a recent high is the traditional definition of a bear market. This describes what the indicators currently show and is for education only, not financial advice. Past signals don’t predict future returns.
