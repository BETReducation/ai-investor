How this works

The map. Each big bubble is a country or region. Its size is the total AI market value of its stocks, its colour is the average return for the period you pick, and the blue ring is its Market Temperature. The small dots around it are the individual stocks. Tap a bubble to see that nation's stocks, and tap it again to go back to the whole world.

Market Temperature (0 to 100). A simple heat score for each stock: 40% how much it has risen over the past year, 30% how close it is to its all-time high, and 30% how expensive it looks on its P/E ratio (loss-making stocks count as fairly expensive). Country and sector scores are averages weighted by market value. It shows how hot something is, not what will happen next.

Which stocks are included. Only AI companies approved by Growth Capital Group (GCG). Core means AI is a meaningful part of the business. AI-adjacent means it supports or is exposed to AI but is not a pure AI play. Companies that simply mention AI are left out.

Colours. Green is up and red is down, brighter for bigger moves. A gold outline or dot means within 5% of its all-time high.

Supply chain lines. Gold lines show who supplies whom, and dimmer ones are partial or indirect. Links marked Checked were confirmed from public reporting. The rest are unverified.

Currency. The currency buttons switch prices, spend and profit between pounds, dollars, euros and yen.

Jargon. P/E is the share price divided by yearly profit per share, and a high number means investors expect lots of growth. Capex is money spent building things like data centres. All-time high (ATH) is the highest price ever reached. Drawdown is how far a price has fallen from that high.

Data. Figures come from the Growth Capital Group (GCG) data feed and may be delayed. This tool is for education only and is not financial advice.
