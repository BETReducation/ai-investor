Snapshot view: latest published annual CPI figures, mostly April to June 2026 (Türkiye is Dec 2025). Inflation is released monthly, so for the latest annual figures use the World Bank button. Countries without a reliable figure are shown in grey, borders are simplified, and Antarctica has no currency.

Exchange rates and the Bitcoin price update live when you are online (otherwise they are an indicative snapshot). Inflation figures are rounded annual averages from public statistics and may be revised. Economy figures (debt, jobs, central bank rates, growth) are rounded estimates drawn from IMF, central bank and national sources for roughly 2025 to mid-2026, and n/a means no clear current figure.
