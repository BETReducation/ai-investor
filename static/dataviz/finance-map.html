🌦 Financial weather guide

☀️ Sunny: debt under 60% of GDP and growth of 3% or more.

🌤 Partly sunny: growth of 2% or more with debt up to 90%.

☁️ Cloudy: everything in between.

🌫 Foggy: inflation above 8%, so prices are hard to see through.

🌧 Rainy: debt over 100% of GDP or growth under 1%.

⛈ Stormy: debt over 100% and growth under 2%.

📖 Learn: what the numbers mean

Debt / GDP: what a government owes compared with the size of its economy. Higher means more of future output goes to repayments.

GDP growth: how fast the economy is expanding after inflation. Growth makes debt easier to carry.

Inflation: how fast prices rise. It erodes savings and pushes central banks to raise rates.

Policy rate: the central bank’s main interest rate. It drives bank lending rates and currency strength.

Real rate: policy rate minus inflation. Positive means savers are rewarded; negative means they are losing purchasing power.

Credit rating: an agency’s view of a government’s ability to repay. AAA is the safest; below BBB- is “junk”.

Resilience score: our own simple 0–100 measure of growth against debt. A learning tool, not an official index.
