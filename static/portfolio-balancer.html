WHY TRACKING YOUR PORTFOLIO WORKS
01
SEE EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
Add each investment you hold — a savings account, an ISA, a crypto wallet, a pension — with its starting value. Instead of checking five different apps, you get one honest total, and one clear gain or loss figure.
02
TOP-UPS DON'T COUNT AS "PROFIT"
When you add more money to an investment, it's tracked separately from market growth. Your gain/loss percentage always compares your current value against what you've actually put in — not against money you just deposited.
03
UPDATE REGULARLY, SPOT DRIFT EARLY
Every value update and top-up is logged with a date, so you can see how each investment has moved over time. Checking in regularly — monthly is plenty — helps you notice if one investment has grown to dominate your portfolio.
