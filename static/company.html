Where this is heading

🗺️

Right now: everything lives on one site, under one nav — Learn, Tools and Arena included. That's deliberate: the group is still small, and splitting things up too early would confuse people before there's anything to gain from it.

Next: as an arm outgrows shared space, it moves to its own subdomain under Growth Capital Group's own domain — e.g. tools.growthcapitalgroup.com, learn.growthcapitalgroup.com, arena.growthcapitalgroup.com — done one arm at a time, whenever it's ready, not all at once.

Eventually: once an arm is genuinely big enough to be its own product, it can move to its own domain entirely. This page — the Group page — becomes the permanent home for governance, partnerships, and anything that sits above the individual arms.
