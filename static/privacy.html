Privacy and cookies

Last updated 6 November 2026.

Analytics

We measure how the site is used with two tools. Cloudflare Web Analytics records page views, referrers and device type without cookies and without tracking you across sites. Google Analytics 4 records similar usage data using cookies, and only runs if you accept the cookie banner. We have switched on IP anonymisation and do not use Google Analytics data for advertising.

Your choice

You can decline or change your choice at any time. Declining has no effect on how the site works.

Account cookies

If you sign in, we set essential cookies to keep you logged in. These don't need consent and aren't used for analytics.
