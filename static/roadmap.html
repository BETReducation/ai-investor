The plan

Four phases between now and the end of September. Each one ships something a user can actually see or use.

Phase 1 · 31 Aug – 6 Sep In progress
Audit & plan the tools
  • Full review of the Backtester and Signals pages — what works end-to-end today, what's half-built
  • Every remaining piece reframed around what it actually solves for a user, not just "finish the code"
  • Produces a prioritised build list for the next two phases
Phase 2 · 7 – 21 Sep Upcoming
Build the core content and tools
  • Learning pages written and published, level by level
  • Backtester and Signals gaps closed against the Phase 1 build list
  • Small, frequent updates shipped rather than one big reveal
Phase 3 · 22 – 29 Sep Upcoming
Harden for launch
  • Scope cut to what's genuinely ready — anything else clearly marked "Coming soon" rather than left half-working
  • Full site pass: navigation, search and links checked end to end
🏁
30 Sep 2026 Target
MVP launch
  • Website live at MVP standard — usable and coherent, not just built

How we're working

The principles behind the plan, in case anyone's wondering why it looks like this.

📣
Progress over meetings
We'd rather ship something small and post it than schedule a call about shipping something.
✂️
Real deadline, real cuts
30 September means something because we're willing to trim scope to hit it, not push the date.
💬
Money follows product
Monetisation gets announced at MVP, and switched on tool by tool as each one earns it — not before.
